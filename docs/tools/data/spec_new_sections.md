## 0. Glossary

| term | meaning |
|---|---|
| Item | One curriculum entry (word or phrase). |
| Way | One of 4 ways to answer: **R-ZE** reading ZH→EN (see or hear Chinese, tap the English meaning), **R-EZ** reading EN→ZH (see English, tap the Chinese), **S-ZE** spoken ZH→EN (hear or see Chinese, say the English), **S-EZ** spoken EN→ZH (see English, say the Chinese). "Reading" means multiple choice. |
| Active ways | 4 when speech works (the default after the consent gate). 2 (R-ZE, R-EZ) only when the browser has no speech recognition or the mic is blocked. |
| Proficient | 2 correct answers (in total) in every active way. At most 1 of the 2 can come from Preview practice. |
| Location | A village, dungeon or stage with a local pool of **47–60 items** (curriculum v2). It **ends in a boss**. The realm's last location has the realm boss. |
| Readiness | The share of the location's own pool that's proficient. It decides boss-approach patrols (§7.4). |
| Carry-over | Up to 20 not-yet-proficient items from earlier locations. They're mixed into battle sets, but not counted in readiness. |
| Role | `normal`, `elite` or `boss`. Bosses have `boss_type` = `location` or `realm`. |
| Tier `t` | Numeric difficulty: realm 1 = 1 … realm 9 = 9, open-ended. All stats are formulas of `t`. |
| G | Gold paid by a normal enemy at tier t: 3 × L_rec(t). Prices are multiples of G. |
| Restore | A full HP+MP refill: a paid inn stay, a free (pity) inn stay, or waking at the inn after a defeat. |
| Preview | The page before each location that lists its new items and offers practice modes (§8). |

@@@ 1.1
### 1.1 Turn order

A **round** = an optional companion attack + 1 hero turn + 1 enemy turn per living enemy on screen (max 3).

```
Round:
  0. Companion   -> if the Friendship gauge is full: free team attack (no question), gauge -> 0   (§6.6)
  1. Hero turn   -> Attack 攻击 | Skills 技能 | Items 道具 (potions) | Run 逃跑 (flee)
                    Attack and question-type skills => 1 question
  2. Enemy phase -> for each living enemy, left to right: wind-up => 1 question => resolve block
                    (Hydra: every 3rd round it attacks twice, §7.9)
  3. End of round -> tick cooldowns, specials (Regrow, Summon Worker), spawn reinforcements (tier 10+), check end
```

- **Defense questions per round = living enemies on screen (≤ 3).** A 3-enemy round is 1 attack question plus 3 defense questions. Extra enemies (tier 10+) wait as reinforcements.
- Potions and Flee use the hero turn **without** a question. Potions are capped at 3 per battle. **Spells (v3.4)** also use the hero turn without a question: at most 1 per normal or elite battle and 2 per boss battle, and only after 3 correct answers in that battle (§6.7). MP potions can't be used in battle.
- **HP and MP carry over between battles.** HP comes back only from an inn, a potion, Heal, or waking at the inn after a defeat. MP also regenerates **+1 per correct answer** in battle (§6.4).

@@@ 1.2
### 1.2 State machine

```
BATTLE_INIT (HP/MP carried over from the map; Friendship gauge = 0)
  -> SCOUT            (the 7 focus items; new items get a teach card; skippable after 1 s)
  -> ROUND_START
  -> COMPANION_CHECK  (gauge >= 100 -> TEAM_ATTACK -> CHECK_END)
  -> HERO_ACTION_SELECT
       (4 bilingual commands: ⚔️ 攻击 Attack · ✨ 技能 Skills · 🍯 道具 Items · 🏃 逃跑 Run)
       Attack/Skill -> TARGET_SELECT -> ASK
       Items (potion) -> APPLY_POTION -> ENEMY_PHASE
       Run          -> END_FLEE
  ASK:
       ENGINE_PICK           (item + way, §3.4; spoken share capped at 50%)
    -> PROMPT_PLAY           (audio/text; options or mic locked)
    -> INPUT_UNLOCKED        (after the audio ends, or 800 ms for text-only prompts)
    -> AWAIT_ANSWER          MC tap | SPEECH_LISTEN -> SPEECH_RESULT
         SPEECH_RESULT = TECH_FAIL (no speech at all / device / permission / network error)
                           -> re-prompt the SAME question (max 2), then VOID (§9.1)
                       = TRANSCRIPT (any recognized text) or NOMATCH -> graded, no retry (nomatch = wrong)
    -> GRADE                 (correct/wrong; proficiency, Leitner, streak; +1 MP if correct;
                              gauge +5 correct / +20 wrong; log)
    -> FEEDBACK              (correct characters + English meaning, zh-CN audio plays; never pinyin;
                              wrong: the correct card lights up, 1.5 s min)
  -> RESOLVE_ATTACK / RESOLVE_BLOCK -> CHECK_END
  -> ENEMY_PHASE ... -> ROUND_END (specials) -> ROUND_START ...

On each enemy reaching HP 0: DROP (EXP + gold now; chest roll, §7.2). Summoned workers drop nothing.
CHECK_END:  all enemies dead (or boss dead: remaining minions flee)  -> VICTORY
            hero HP <= 0 (and no Second Wind)                         -> DEFEAT
            boss HP <= 50% (first time)                               -> BOSS_CHECKPOINT (save only, NO heal)
VICTORY  -> REWARDS (EXP, gold, chest contents, proficiency summary) -> MAP (HP/MP as they are)
DEFEAT   -> INN: full HP/MP, lose gold per §7.5 (keep items, EXP and drops already collected);
            boss: next attempt starts at 50% boss HP if the checkpoint was reached
END_FLEE -> MAP (no loss; answers so far still count)
```

**Battle UI rules (v3.2, decided by Jack).** Layout reference: `/workspace/desy/ui-layout-review.md` (§4 battle screen, §5 turn-state flow).

- **Commands are bilingual.** Each of the 4 command buttons shows the Chinese label with the English under or beside it: **⚔️ 攻击 Attack · ✨ 技能 Skills · 🍯 道具 Items · 🏃 逃跑 Run**. The Chinese is free incidental vocabulary and isn't graded. Skills, Items and target choice open as lists inside the dock, with a ◀ Back button.
- **Answer feedback is audio, never pinyin.** Correct: the tapped card turns green and the word's zh-CN audio plays; the message line shows the characters + English meaning (e.g. "✔ 您好 = hello (polite)"). Wrong: the picked card shakes with ✘, the correct card lights green, its zh-CN audio plays, and the line shows the correct characters + English meaning ("Not quite. “hello (polite)” is 您好 🔊"). This is held for at least 1.5 s.
- **No pinyin anywhere in the game UI:** not in prompts, options, feedback, enemy names, the Preview/word list, flashcards or results. Pinyin stays in the data for speech matching (§9.2), polyphone checks and the parent editor (behind the parent gate).

@@@ 3.1
### 3.1 Definition

Per item, the engine tracks each **way** separately:

```
WayStat { correct: int; attempts: int; runLen: int /* current correct run */;
          practiceCredit: 0|1 /* §8: at most 1 correct from Preview practice */;
          box: 0..6; lastSeen; lastPromo; dueAt /* Leitner, scheduling only */ }
Item progress = { R-ZE, R-EZ, S-ZE, S-EZ : WayStat }
```

- **Speech is expected to be on.** At first launch a parent sees a consent screen: the mic is used push-to-talk only; Chrome sends the audio to Google for recognition; transcripts, never audio, are stored locally. The game doesn't start until they accept. After that, `activeWays` = all 4.
- **2-way path (reading only):** `activeWays = [R-ZE, R-EZ]` only when the browser has no `SpeechRecognition` (Safari/Firefox/iPad), or a `not-allowed` / `service-not-allowed` / `language-not-supported` error disables speech (§9.1). The HUD says "Reading mode: this browser can't listen." This is a fallback, not an option in settings.
- **Proficient(item)** = every active way has `correct ≥ 2`, counted **in total, not in a row** (decided).
- **Practice credit:** `correct` in a way may include at most **1** correct answer from Preview practice (`practiceCredit`). The 2nd must come from a graded battle question (§8.3).
- **Way progress** = `min(correct, 2)`. **Item progress** = the sum over active ways ÷ (2 × active ways), from 0 to 1. The HUD shows up to 8 pips (4 in reading mode).
- **Hints:** a correct answer given after the Insight hint doesn't add to `correct`.
- **`nomatch` = wrong** (decided, §9.1).
- **Speech turned on later** (e.g. the kid moves from an iPad to a Chromebook): items that are already proficient under 2 ways **stay proficient** (decided). Their spoken ways start at 0 and are scheduled like review (weight 0.15 + overdue boost). They don't count against readiness and don't trigger patrols. Items that aren't yet proficient simply gain 2 more ways to fill.

**No rusty rule** (decided): once proficient, an item stays proficient. Wrong answers on proficient items only move the Leitner box, which brings the item back sooner.

@@@ 3.4
### 3.4 Picking the way for each question

```python
SPOKEN_CAP = 0.50                                                   # max share of spoken questions per battle

def pick_way(item):
    ways = kid.active_ways()
    if battle.spoken_asked + 1 > SPOKEN_CAP * max(battle.asked + 1, 4):   # cap reached: reading ways only
        ways = [w for w in ways if w.startswith('R')]
    todo = [w for w in ways if item[w].correct < 2]                 # the weakest ways first
    if todo:
        return min(todo, key=lambda w: (item[w].correct,             # fewest corrects
                                        est_acc(item[w]),            # then lowest (c+1)/(a+2)
                                        random()))
    return max(ways, key=lambda w: (overdue(item[w]), -est_acc(item[w]), random()))   # proficient: most overdue way
```

- **Spoken cap (decided, 50%):** without it, speech-on kids got about 56% spoken questions. With it they get 48–50%, and in-battle accuracy rises about 1 point. The cost is about 15–20% more patrols but only about 5% more time (e.g. t1 at 75%: 8.9 vs 7.5 patrols, 41.6 vs 39.3 min per location), because the spoken ways fill a bit more slowly. Defeat rates barely move (`data/sim/sim_spoken_cap.csv`).
- Presentation per way:
  - **R-ZE:** characters + audio (for `reading = N` items the audio is the main stimulus), English options. No pinyin.
  - **R-EZ:** English prompt; Chinese options as characters only (no pinyin, v3.2). The correct answer's audio plays in the feedback.
  - **S-ZE:** Chinese audio + characters; the kid says the English meaning (`en-US` recognizer).
  - **S-EZ:** English prompt plus a picture if available; the kid says the Chinese (`zh-CN` recognizer).
- The first-ever question on an item uses one fewer MC option (minimum 2).

@@@ 7.4
### 7.4 Boss-approach patrols (extra encounters)

**What:** the zone just before each location boss. While **readiness** is below the trigger, **patrols** (normal enemies of the location's roster) walk the approach path.

**Readiness (v3)** = proficient items ÷ items in **the location's own pool** (47–60). Carried-over items (up to 20 earlier, not-yet-proficient items) are still mixed into every battle set, but they **no longer count** in readiness. In v2 they did, so a kid who struggled in location 1 dragged that debt into every later gate. With the larger v2 pools, the own-pool denominator cuts patrols by about 20% (t1 at 75%, speech on, light practice, 6 path fights, both at a 25% trigger: 16.6 → 12.9 patrols). The HUD at the gate shows "Ready words: 9 / 52 (need 11)".

**Default trigger (decided):** **20%** of the location pool with speech on (Director's call in v3.1, down from 25%), **40%** in reading mode.

**How it plays:**

1. Walking from the last path fight to the boss gate while below the trigger, the kid meets **2 patrols** ("each approach trip"). They can't be sneaked past. This is the only forced cost.
2. After those 2, the **boss gate is always open**. The kid chooses **Enter** (fight the boss now) or **Train**, which spawns another patrol right away and is repeatable.
3. **Elite patrols:** each patrol has a **10%** chance to include the location's elite in place of one normal enemy. If the location has no elite of its own, it uses the realm's elite. The elite glows gold on the map, so the kid can see it's worth more (2 × G, 25% chest).
4. When readiness reaches the trigger, patrols disappear (one optional wandering patrol stays for farming), and the gate glows "Ready!".
5. If the kid loses to the boss, they wake at the inn. Walking back to the gate triggers up to 2 more patrols if still below the trigger.
6. Patrol fights use the normal 7-item selection, which already favors the least-proficient items. So patrols target exactly what's missing.
7. Preview practice (§8) raises readiness before the kid even reaches the gate. Light practice cuts patrols by about 25–30%, and full practice by about 50–57%.

**It never blocks the boss.**

### 8.3 Practice modes and the counting rule

{{table:practice_modes}}

**Counting rule (decided):** a correct practice answer counts toward proficiency, but **at most 1 of the 2 correct answers per way** can come from practice (`WayStat.practiceCredit`, 0 or 1). Details:
- Only graded modes count (listen-and-pick, say-it, matching, quick quiz). Flashcards are self-marked and never count.
- Practice uses the same matching rules as battle (§9.2), `nomatch` = wrong, and no hints.
- A wrong practice answer doesn't remove credit and doesn't move the Leitner box. Practice has **no penalty**.
- Practice doesn't mark an item "seen" for the battle engine. NEW_CAP and the working set still decide when items enter battle sets, so a kid can't skip the teach-card flow.
- Sessions are short: 8–12 questions per round, then a "Go fight!" button. No timers (decided). Matching is untimed and counts moves, not seconds.

What the sim models: "light" practice = one R-ZE (listen-and-pick) + one S-EZ (say-it) per item (R-ZE + R-EZ in reading mode). "Full" = each active way once per item. Both use the ≤1-per-way cap. Results are in §7.7: light cuts patrols about 25–30% (reading mode: ~80%) at about the same total time; full cuts about 50–57%.

### 8.4 Rewards (small, and never better than battle farming)

{{table:practice_rewards}}

Why it can't beat farming: practice pays 0 gold and 0 EXP per question. The one-time "Preview complete" reward per location (1 Honey Potion + 1 × G) is worth about 2 normal enemies, and it's only given once. Stickers and the realm cosmetic are purely cosmetic, stored in a sticker book. Proficiency EXP (+10 per way completed, +30 per proficient item) is paid only when the way is completed in battle, even if practice supplied the first correct answer.

**Rules (v3.4, Jack 2026-10-02 PT).**
- **Shop.** Every town has a magic shop 魔法店. Towns 2–9 sell 2 spells each, 16 in total, at 45–130 × G of the selling town (540–9,360 gold). Town 1 sells Mana Tea only.
- **Ownership.** Spells are owned for good. They live in a **Spellbook tab inside ✨ 技能 Skills**, so there are still 4 battle commands and no skill slot is used.
- **Casting is a free action.** It uses the hero's turn but asks **no question** and can't fizzle. The MP is always spent. That turn gives no MP regen and doesn't change the streak. Enemy turns still ask their block questions as usual.
- **Damage:** `max(1, round(P × (Tired ? 1.5 : 1) − DEF_e))`.
  - P is the spell's fixed **power**. It doesn't grow with ATK, gear or level.
  - Spells subtract the enemy's **full DEF**, where attacks subtract 0.5 × DEF.
  - There's no streak or spoken bonus, because no answer is given.
  - Design rule: `P = round(F × HP_normal(t) + DEF(t))`, where F is the share of a normal enemy's HP the spell removes in its own town's realm (0.35–0.95).
  - Normal-enemy HP grows from 27 (t2) to 73 (t9). So each spell drops to the level of a plain attack 2–3 tiers after its town (see `spell_falloff` below).
- **MP cost:** `MP = round(K × MP_pool(t))` with K = 0.33–0.48, using MP_pool = 10 + 2 × L_rec (unchanged). From a full bar, a kid can cast 2.1–3.0 spells at the spell's own tier, or about 1–1.7 if they keep 12 MP for Heal.
  - **MP growth is unchanged.** A kid 3 levels over the recommended level still gets only 2.3–3.4 casts.
  - Old spells become cheap relative to the pool, but by then they're weak.
- **Cast rule (anti-dodge):**
  - **1 cast per normal or elite battle, 2 per boss battle.** The 2nd boss cast needs at least 12 more questions after the first.
  - The Spellbook **unlocks only after 3 correct answers in that battle** (the "magic charge"). So spells are earned by answering, and a fight can't be opened with a free cast.
- **MP potions are expensive and map-only** (they're never drunk in battle, so a potion never replaces a question turn):
  - Mana Tea 魔力茶: +50% max MP for **6 × G** (= 6 normal kills; was 1 × G).
  - Big Mana Tea 大魔力茶: full MP for **15 × G**, from town 5.
  - The inn (2 × G, full HP + MP) stays the cheap refill; tea is the price of not walking back.
- **Magic Ward dropped** (it was ×0.5 spell damage in boss battles in v3.3). With the cast cap and fixed power, the strongest owned spell does only 11–14% of a realm boss's HP from tier 3 on (29% at tier 2). Spells shorten boss fights by about 1 question on average, and first-try win rates don't change. With the Ward, kids would cast half as often on bosses, so spells would "feel broken" there.
- **Unchanged from v3.3:**
  - targeting: single / same type / all on screen, max 3;
  - bosses get half the chance of status effects;
  - the Blacksmith-first warning;
  - spells never combine with Frost or Sweep on the same turn;
  - the companion team attack.

### 7.11 Quests (new in v3.3)

Every town has a quest board 任务板 with **4 quests**: one of each type. Quests can't be repeated, and they're all optional. Rewards are in G of that town, so each realm's board pays about 30 × G in total (36–2,106 gold). Data: `data/quests.json` / `.csv` (36 quests).

| type | goal | reward | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| bounty 打败 | defeat 8 of the realm's first normal enemy | 6 × G + 1 Honey Potion | any fight in the realm counts, boss helpers included |
| collect 收集 | collect 5 drops (e.g. 5片树叶) from the realm's second normal enemy | 8 × G | 35% drop per kill; quest items take no bag slot |
| words 学会 | get 15 of this realm's words to Ready | 10 × G + 1 Mana Tea (worth 6 × G from v3.4) | battle or practice; the quest links to Preview & Practice (§8) |
| delivery 送信 | after the realm boss, take a letter to the next town | 6 × G (paid there) + Return Feather | realm 9: back to 小山村, a cosmetic postman cap |

{{table:quests}}

**Sim.** Savers finish all 4 quests in almost every realm (collect 80–100%). Spenders always finish the bounty and usually the collect quest, but they finish the words quest only in realms 1 and 4 (0–13% elsewhere): it's the quest that rewards slowing down. Quests are about 15% of all gold income.

**How quests feed spells.** Quests pay about 30 × G per realm, which is about 40–55% of a beginner spell or 25% of a top spell. A spell costs 45–130 kills. With that realm's 4 quests done, it costs 18–101 kills. In play time, that's about 1 to 2 h of a saver's realm (57–113 min at the realm's pace).
