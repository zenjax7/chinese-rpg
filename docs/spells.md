For Jack (via Director). Written 2026-10-01 (PT). This builds on combat spec v3.2, the roster (`enemies.json`), the curriculum and the v3.2 sim. The short spec version is in `combat-spec.md` §6.7 (Spells) and §7.11 (Quests), spec v3.3.

## 1. Summary

- **16 spells.** Towns 2–9 each sell 2 spells in their magic shop 魔法店. Town 1 sells Mana Tea and shows a "coming soon" spell shelf. They run from 小火球术 Small Fireball (town 2) to 超暴风雪术 Super Blizzard and 流星雨术 Meteor Shower (town 9). Targeting: single, same type, or all on screen (max 3). Spells are owned for good.
- **Very expensive.** A spell costs 45–130 normal kills at the town that sells it (540–9,360 gold). With that realm's quests done, it costs 18–101 kills. That's 1 to 2 hours of play per spell for a 75% saver. A 75% saver ends the game with 5 of the 16 spells, a 75% spender with 2, and a 50% saver with about 1.
- **Casting = answering.** A spell replaces your attack and asks the normal engine question. Right answer: the spell goes off. Wrong answer: it fizzles and the MP is spent (default, the same as Heal today). Spells don't touch the 50% spoken cap or the 4-way item selection.
- **Two guard rails were needed, both found in the sim:**
  1. **Magic Ward 魔法护盾:** spells do half damage in boss battles. Without it, realm 5–9 boss fights shrink to about 19 questions (target 22–30). With it, boss length and first-try wins match the game without spells.
  2. **Blacksmith first 先买装备:** the magic shop warns when a purchase would leave less gold than the next town's gear. Without it, about 14% of battles are fought in old gear (4% without spells). If kids buy spells *before* gear, defeats jump: 65% saver 1.3% → 12.5%.
- **With both guard rails:**
  - Learning time is unchanged for savers (17.7 → 17.6 h at 75%), because location gates are readiness-based.
  - Defeat rates and boss first-try win rates are unchanged.
  - Normal fights are about 2 questions shorter.
  - Free inns and the gold floor are unaffected: gold never hit 0 in the default config.
  - Spells finally give late-game gold a use. Without spells, a 75% saver hoards about 27,000 unspent gold by the end.
- **Quests:** each town's board has 4 one-time quests (bounty, collect, words, delivery), paying about 30 × G per realm (≈15% of gold income). Savers finish nearly all of them. Spenders skip the words quest, which is the point: it rewards slowing down.

## 2. Rules

| rule | proposal |
|---|---|
| Where | A magic shop 魔法店 in every town. Towns 2–9 sell 2 spells each (one cheaper, one stronger). Town 1 sells Mana Tea and has a "coming soon" shelf (open question). |
| Price | `price = price_G × G(town)`. G is gold per normal enemy in that town's realm (3 × L_rec = 6, 12, 18, 27, 36, 45, 54, 63, 72). price_G is 45–130. |
| Ownership | Permanent. A spell can be cast in any later battle (and back in earlier realms) whenever MP ≥ cost. No levels and no upgrades. |
| Where in battle | **Spellbook 魔法书 tab inside ✨ 技能 Skills.** Spells don't take skill slots, and the battle keeps its 4 commands (⚔️ 攻击 · ✨ 技能 · 🍯 道具 · 🏃 逃跑). The Skills panel gets two tabs: 技能 Skills | 魔法 Spells. Spells you can't afford with your MP are greyed out, and each spell shows the MP left after casting. |
| Turn | Casting replaces the attack. One question is chosen by the learning engine as usual (§1.3). The spell's name isn't the question. |
| Right answer | The spell hits all its targets. The streak goes +1 and you get +1 MP (as usual). |
| Wrong answer | **Fizzle 失灵: no effect, and the MP is spent** (default, same as Heal). Alternative for Jack: a fizzle costs no MP (§7). |
| Damage | `max(1, round(M × ATK_h × (spoken ? 1.25 : 1) × (Tired ? 1.5 : 1) × (Ward ? 0.5 : 1) − 0.5 × DEF_e))`, the same style as the attack formula (§2.2). **No streak multiplier**: a long streak makes plain attacks better, so attacking stays a real choice. |
| Targets | **single**: the tapped enemy. **same type**: every enemy of the tapped enemy's kind (a boss is its own kind, and its helpers share one kind). **all**: every enemy on screen. Max 3 in every case. |
| Statuses | Soaked 湿透 (its next attack does half damage). Dazed 晕 / Chilled 冷 (chance to skip its next attack). Frozen 冻住 (skips its next attack). **Bosses resist**: half chance. |
| Magic Ward | In location and realm boss battles, every enemy (helpers included) takes ×0.5 spell damage. Shown as a soft violet shimmer on the boss and its helpers ("魔法护盾!" the first time). |
| Blacksmith first | If a spell purchase would leave less gold than the next town's weapon + armor + shield, the shopkeeper says "先买装备吧?" (gear first?) and the kid must confirm. |
| Elements | Cosmetic for now (fire, water, ice, thunder, wind, light, star). No weaknesses (open question). |

How they grow: each town sells one "big single-target" spell and one "crowd" spell.
- Single: 1.6 → 2.0 → 2.2 → 2.6 → 3.0 → 3.5.
- Same type: 1.0 → 1.3 → 1.6 → 2.0.
- All: 0.8 → 1.1 → 1.3 → 1.6/2.0.

MP costs grow with the MP pool (10 + 2L: 18 MP at town 2, 58 MP at town 9). A spell costs 35–70% of the pool, so a kid casts about 1–2 spells per battle (sim: 1.46 per battle at 75%) and has to think about what to keep for Heal or Shield.

Chinese names: every name is "X + 术" (术 = magic art, the usual kid-fantasy word: 火球术 is what Chinese kids' games call a fireball). They build on curriculum words wherever possible: 雪, 风, 雨, 闪电, 太阳, 星星, 打雷 (雷), 大/小, and 火 (known from 火车). So reading the spell name is itself practice. 阳光, 流星 and 泡泡 aren't in the curriculum, but they're transparent compounds of known characters, or very common kid words. 泡泡 (HSK 6 on paper) is very common in kids' speech. 超 and 暴 are the hardest characters. 超暴风雪术 is Jack's own example and stays as is.

## 4. How spells fit with skills, slots and question rules

| existing | interaction with spells |
|---|---|
| ⚔️ Attack | Still the default. A spell only beats an attack when it hits more enemies or the streak is low. With a ×1.5–2.0 streak, plain attacks are often as good as a single-target spell, and they're free. |
| Insight 提示 (4 MP) | Can be used on the spell's question as on any MC question. As usual, it counts in combat but not for proficiency. |
| Double Strike 连击 | Attacks only. It doesn't apply to spells. |
| Guardian Shield 守护盾 (8 MP, auto) | Unchanged. Spells compete with it for MP. The sim's kid keeps 8 MP for it until it has fired. Suggestion: the Spellbook shows "MP left after the cast". |
| Heal 治疗 (12 MP) | The same "answer to cast, fizzle spends MP" rule, so the fizzle default is consistent. Spells take MP that Heal would use. In the sim, heals per battle drop (0.11 → 0.04 at 75%) mostly because fights are shorter; defeat rates don't move. |
| Frost 冰冻 (t8+) / Sweep 横扫 (t7+) | These are attack modifiers, so they can't be combined with a spell on the same turn (a spell replaces the attack). Overlap: Sweep (0.6 × all) is cheaper than 雪花术/暴风雪术, and Frost (a sure freeze on one enemy) is cheaper than a status spell. They stay useful because they cost 8–12 MP against 16–40 for a spell. |
| Second Wind 再起 (20 MP, auto, t9) | Unchanged. The sim's kid keeps 20 MP for it in realm 9, so late spells (34–40 MP) need a big MP pool or Mana Tea. |
| Companion team attack | Unchanged. It's a free action and doesn't interact with spells. Friendship rises on wrong answers, fizzles included. |
| Skill slots | Spells don't use skill slots (recommended). They're bought, not equipped, and a separate menu keeps the 3 skill slots meaningful. The alternative ("a spell takes a skill slot") would force kids to give up Heal or Shield, which hurts weaker kids. |
| 50% spoken cap | No change. The spell's question is an ordinary engine question, so it counts toward the cap like any other. |
| 4-way item selection | No change. The engine picks the item and the way (R-ZE, R-EZ, S-ZE, S-EZ) as usual. The spell only changes what happens on a right answer. |
| Tired (30 / 45 q) | Spells get the same ×1.5 hero damage bonus once enemies are Tired. |
| Gold for kills | Enemies defeated by a spell pay gold, EXP and chest rolls as usual, and count for bounty and collect quests. |

## 5. MP economy

- **Sources (unchanged):** +1 MP per correct answer, a full refill at the inn and on waking after a defeat, and Mana Tea 魔力茶 (+50% max MP, 1 × G, uses a turn in battle).
- **Pool (unchanged):** 10 + 2L. At the recommended level that's 18 MP in realm 2 and 58 in realm 9.
- **What the sim shows:**
  - Spells give MP a real job. Before v3.3, a 75% kid mostly sat on full MP after Shield (Heal 0.11 per battle). Now about 1.5 spells are cast per battle at 75%.
  - Bosses aren't trivialized, thanks to the Ward.
  - MP isn't pointless: at 50% accuracy almost half of all casts fizzle, so MP budgeting really matters for weaker kids.
- **Mana Tea:** keep the price at 1 × G. The words quest gives one free tea per realm. The sim's saver keeps one tea once she owns spells, and drinks it between fights when MP < 40%.
- **No pool growth change proposed.** The top spells (34–40 MP) take 60–70% of a realm-9 pool, so a kid can cast one, maybe two, per battle. That's the intended "big moment" feel. If Jack wants more casting, the simplest knob is spell MP cost, not the pool.

## 7. Sim results

**Model.** A whole campaign is simulated: realms 1–9 in a row, with gold, potions, Mana Tea, spells and words carried over. 150 runs per row, using the v3.2 learning model (speech on unless noted, NEW_CAP 3, Tired 30/45, 8 path fights, patrols to 20% readiness, companion, 50% spoken cap).
- **Gear:** the kid enters each realm in the previous tier's gear and buys weapon (+3 ATK), armor (+2 DEF) and shield (+1 DEF) in town.
- **Skills:** Heal and Shield from realm 2, Sweep from 7, Frost from 8, Second Wind in 9.
- **Kid profiles:**
  - **Saver:** keeps 2 inns of gold in reserve and 1 Mana Tea.
  - **Spender:** keeps no reserve and goes to the boss early.
  - **Both:** buy inns and potions as in v3.2, then gear, then wish for the most expensive spell of the current town. They cast a spell when it's worth at least 1.25× a plain attack, keeping MP for Shield, Heal (below 50% HP) and Second Wind.

**Configurations:**
- "no spells": v3.2 rules (gold just piles up).
- "spells": the defaults (Ward + Blacksmith-first warning, MP spent on a fizzle).
- "refund": a fizzle costs no MP.
- "no Ward/nudge" and "no nudge": the guard rails removed.
- "spell-first": the kid ignores the warning and buys spells before gear.
- "v3.2 cosmetics": surplus gold goes to cosmetics.

How to read it:
- **Learning time.** Savers' playthrough time doesn't move, because locations are readiness-gated. Spenders play about 7% less (12.8 → 11.9 h at 75%), because their fights are shorter and they leave early anyway. That's 5–6 fewer minutes per location in realms 6–9.
- **Safety nets.** Free inns stay ≤ 0.2 per run in the default config, and gold-zero events stay at 0.
- **Gold floor.** The lowest gold a 75% saver reaches drops (514 → 465 median), but stays far above an inn.
- **Guard rails matter.** Without the nudge, the full-gear share falls to 0.86. With "spell-first", it's 0.07–0.14, and defeats rise to 2.8% (saver 75%), 7.4% (spender 75%) and 12.5% (saver 65%).
- **Spells aren't a crutch at 50%.** They neither rescue nor hurt struggling kids much (defeats 11.5% → 12.0%). A 50% spender with the nudge never affords a spell at all.

What this says about "very expensive":
- **The first spell comes easily.** Realm 1 has almost nothing to spend gold on (shield 35g, inns). A 75% saver arrives in town 2 with about 750 gold and can afford 小火球术 (720) almost on arrival. She buys it about 1.3 h later, after gear.
- **After that, savers buy about one spell in most realms.** Usually in realms 4, 6, 7 and 9 (78–97% of runs), and in realms 3, 5 and 8 in only 16–36% of runs. Each comes 1–2 h after reaching the town.
- **Spenders get about 2 spells per game.** 旋风术 in realm 4 (96% of runs) and 超暴风雪术 in realm 9 (91%).
- **A 50% saver gets about 1.** Usually 小火球术.

If Jack wants the first spell to feel earned, raise the town-2 prices to about 90 × G (1,080), or give town 1 a gold sink.
